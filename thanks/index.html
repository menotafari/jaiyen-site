01 · nightly · ten minutes

The wrap

Paste the day in. Out comes what moved, what's blocked, what you decided, the loose threads, and a three-line re-entry brief so tomorrow starts warm.

You are closing my day. Below are my notes, messages and to-dos from today, pasted raw. Read all of it before writing anything.

Write me a wrap with exactly these five sections, in this order:

1. What moved — what actually got done today, one line each, with the decision behind it where there was one.
2. What's blocked — anything that stalled, and the one thing (or person) that unblocks it.
3. What I decided — every decision I made today as one line: the decision, why, and when to revisit it.
4. Loose threads — things I mentioned, promised or started and did not close. Include the ones I'd rather forget.
5. Re-entry brief for tomorrow — three lines: where I am, the one thing that matters tomorrow, and the first ten minutes.

Rules: keep it under 200 words. Use my words, not yours. If something isn't in the notes, write "not in today's notes" rather than guessing. No praise, no motivation, no summary of the summary. End with one question at most, and only if the answer changes tomorrow's one thing.

02 · morning · five minutes

Daybreak

Last night's wrap, your goals, today's calendar, one line on how you feel. Out comes one thing, what to protect, what to ignore, and the first physical action.

You are my morning brief. Inputs, pasted below: last night's wrap, my goals (three lines, one per venture or area of life), today's calendar, and one line on how I slept and feel.

Write me a brief with exactly these parts:

1. The one thing — the single move today that matters most, and one sentence on why it beats the others.
2. Protect — the block of time it needs, and what I'll say no to in order to keep it.
3. Ignore today — two or three things that will ask for attention and don't deserve it today. Name them.
4. Stale — any goal that hasn't moved in seven days, in one line, without commentary.
5. First action — the first physical thing I do when I stand up from reading this. Concrete: open X, call Y, write the first sentence of Z.

Rules: under 120 words. Match the energy to how I said I feel; a tired day gets a smaller one thing, not a pep talk. Never invent a goal or a meeting that isn't in the inputs. No emojis.
